Caring for the soil is also profitable

score = suitability × (w₁·income + w₂·soil health + w₃·input savings + w₄·resilience + w₅·compliance)

Income

30 %

Margin from expected yield, price and cost

Soil health

25 %

Residue, erosion cover, roots, crop diversity

Input savings

15 %

Nitrogen fixed by legumes and cover crops

Resilience

15 %

Tolerance to the zone’s drought and flooding

Compliance

15 %

Environmental rules, such as no endless monoculture

The farmer adjusts the weights. No component can exceed 60 %.

Our core message: caring for the land is not only ecological, it is profitable. A depleted soil gives more today and less later. The score mixes income with soil health, so cover crops and rotations can compete with commercial crops. Weights are defaults and the farmer can change them.
