Challenge · Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data

01 · The problem

Every season is a bet.

A
The weather is driftingTemperatures shift, rain gets less predictable, water runs short.
B
Today’s yield, tomorrow’s soilSqueezing a plot for one harvest can degrade the soil and hurt the cycles after it.
C
The data exists, the access doesn’tSatellite and climate data are hard to use, and mostly reach large companies.
