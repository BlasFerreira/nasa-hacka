Each square is one hectare (100 × 100 m) of the real plot. Soil variation between hectares is illustrative: it starts from the plot parameters and changes smoothly. Climate (NASA POWER) and yields, prices and costs (MAGyP) are real. Tap a square to see the details.

ⓘ About the map data

Sample farms: the boundaries follow real roads and paddocks from OpenStreetMap, but the plots and their data are fictional. Climate comes from the NASA POWER cell (0.5° × 0.625°, about 55 × 60 km): it is the same for every plot inside it.
